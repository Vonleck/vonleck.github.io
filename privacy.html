Blackhat Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026
Developer: Aapo Virola ("I", "me")
Contact: aapo.virola@gmail.com

1. Summary

Blackhat is an offline single-player game. I do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, upgrades, settings and similar) is saved only in the app's storage on your device. I cannot see it. If you use Android or Google device backup, Google may include the app's data in your backup under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes the data from your device.

3. Purchases

The optional "Blackhat Pro" unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles payment, billing and purchase records under its own privacy policy. I do not receive your name, email, payment details or purchase history. The app only asks Google Play whether the Pro unlock is owned on your account.

4. Permissions and network

The app declares internet access because Google Play Billing needs it. The game itself does not send your data anywhere and contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

5. Children

Blackhat is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Because I do not process your personal data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU/EEA you have rights under the GDPR, and you can contact me with any question or complaint. You may also contact your national data protection authority (in Finland: the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman).

7. Fiction

Blackhat is a fictional game for entertainment. It contains no real hacking instructions, tools or targets.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date.
